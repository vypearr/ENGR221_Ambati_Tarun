get_first_cell_ai() - main idea is from where i am right now, what nearby cells can i travel to until i reach a duck

set current to the food cell which was found by the search

while there are still cells to search
remove one cell from the queue/stack
and 
check if its food
if yes, find out the first step toward it
if no, look at its neighboring cells(north,east,south,west) and see if it has been searched yet
if the neighboring cells are not apart of the players body, then mark its search parent as the current cell and add it to the queue/stack
